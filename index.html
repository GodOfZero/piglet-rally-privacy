Privacy Policy — Piglet Rally

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Piglet Rally (“the Game”) handles information. The Game is published by Oleksandr Matiashov, individual entrepreneur (FOP), Ichnia, Ukraine (“we”, “us”).

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any account. The Game has no sign-in.

  • Game progress (levels, in-game currency, settings, language) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it. Deleting the Game deletes this data.
  • Gameplay statistics used for balancing are kept on your device and are not sent to us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, approximate location (derived from IP), and ad interaction data. This processing is done by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the Game: Settings → Privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. We receive only the information needed to confirm the purchase.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with Google (advertising and app stores) as described above, or when required by law.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, most requests are handled through the controls described above and through Google. For any question, contact us.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address, with its effective date.

8. Contact

Oleksandr Matiashov, Ichnia, Chernihiv Oblast, Ukraine — matiashovsasha@gmail.com
